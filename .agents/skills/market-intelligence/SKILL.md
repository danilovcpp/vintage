# Competitive intelligence

Read `playbooks/EVIDENCE_AND_RESEARCH.md`; use market_researcher and product_strategist as read-only experts when supported. Define geography, customer segment, problem category and decision being made. Research direct competitors, adjacent substitutes, OSS, manual/no-product routes. Verify capabilities, pricing and limitations from dated primary sources; supplement with independent sources for user experience.

Create `deliverables/02-opportunity-assessment.md` from template if this work is part of viability stage. Include competitor matrix, opportunity gaps, positioning hypothesis, citations, research caveats and evidence grade. Record external evidence in `workspace/SOURCES.md`. Market sizing needs explicit bottom-up method and uncertainty; do not invent TAM numbers. If web unavailable, mark work `PRELIMINARY — RESEARCH NOT VERIFIED` and provide search/interview plan. If internal-only platform, focus on build vs buy rather than synthetic competitive dynamics.
