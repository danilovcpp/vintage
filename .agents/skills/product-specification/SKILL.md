# Product specification

Input: owner-confirmed `PROJECT_BRIEF`, question/decision logs and viability output. Use `templates/03_PRODUCT_SPEC.md`. Define audience, JTBD, workflows, business rules, MVP and explicit exclusions; assign goal `GOAL-*`, persona `PER-*`, journey `JRN-*`, feature/requirement `FR-*`. Prioritize using value, cost and risk; not just MoSCoW labels without explanation. Write acceptance by observable business behavior, not architecture technology.

Attach decision/source provenance and known assumptions to requirements. Explain what is deliberately deferred. Reconcile with independent business analyst review. Mark DRAFT, request owner review on scope. Owner explicit approval only; don't silently jump to technical implementation.
