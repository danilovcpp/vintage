# Business case and feasibility

Use when judging ROI, business model, monetization or whether software should exist at all. Gather inputs from owner and sources, distinguish actual figures vs assumptions. Assess implementation, infrastructure, licences, marketing/acquisition, operations/support, switching and maintenance.

Produce base, low and high cases (with transparent formulas), drivers of break-even and sensitivity; for internal software assess cost avoided and adoption. Include non-financial blockers, technical feasibility, time-to-value and build/buy/no-build alternatives. Recommend GO, PIVOT, NO-GO or MORE-EVIDENCE with confidence and smallest validation experiment. Do not misrepresent uncertain income as forecast fact. Collaborate with financial_analyst and independent_reviewer when needed.
