# Evidence and research rules

- Use primary sources when possible: vendor pricing, official docs, regulator text, customer interviews (when provided), repository evidence. For popularity/opinion use multiple independent sources.
- For each material external claim store exact URL, publisher, publication date (if known), verification date, evidence strength (primary/secondary/anecdote), possible bias.
- Competitor matrix columns: who, positioning, audience, features, pricing (currency + plan + date), limits, strengths/weaknesses, differentiator, citations. Mark unknowns rather than approximating them as facts.
- Compare direct, adjacent, indirect (manual/spreadsheets), and non-consumption alternatives. Capture regional context and pricing model.
- Do not present TAM/SAM/SOM as precise numeric facts absent a defensible methodology; prefer bottoms-up assumptions, ranges and falsification tests.
- For internal tooling, narrow research to build/buy and relevant vendor/OSS alternatives; skip vanity market sizing.
- Regulations are jurisdiction-specific, time-sensitive. Verify current official text, note uncertainties; do not represent as legal opinion.
- When internet is inaccessible, produce **Research plan / preliminary hypotheses**, not a completed sourced competitor study. Update `workspace/SOURCES.md`.
- Never paste secrets/proprietary customer data into public search or external SaaS without explicit consent.
